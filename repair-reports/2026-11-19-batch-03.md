## Source/licensing research
- Seek's Cool Deltarune GameBanana page lists CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and marks its Psych Online port as obsolete/archival: https://gamebanana.com/mods/621679
- Vs Henry Stickmin source/download pages found are desktop Psych Engine mods with CC BY-NC-ND 4.0; no complete HTML5/offline build was found: https://gamebanana.com/mods/378316 and https://gamebanana.com/mods/445367
- Super Mario Bros Funkers is a Mario-derived fan mod. Its GameBanana page lists CC BY 4.0 for the creator's work, but the download is a Psych Engine archive, not a complete local web build; Nintendo's Mario assets are third-party copyrighted: https://gamebanana.com/mods/463482
- The All-Stars search found a fan collection page with CC BY-NC-ND terms, but not an exact authorized standalone web build for the folder's version: https://gamebanana.com/mods/361304
- No legitimate offline source/license for the Packman mod was found.

## Decision
These were incomplete external web launchers, not complete offline builds. No authorized, complete, locally runnable web source was found during this search. They were removed rather than kept as broken placeholders or rehosted from third-party game portals.
